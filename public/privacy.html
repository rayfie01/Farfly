Privacy policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Far.Fly is an independent visual discovery and music project maintained by rayfie01. This policy covers farfly.vercel.app.

Current status: The public app uses a demo collection. Pinterest and SoundCloud account connections are not currently active. The Pinterest section below describes the proposed integration, which will require your explicit authorization before it accesses your account.

Information used by the current app

Your saved visuals, likes, saved mood sessions, selected boards and recommendation preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. Recent interactions help suggest music within the app. These themes are not a diagnosis or an assessment of your mental health. Demo image analysis runs locally; the app does not send images or browsing activity to an AI service.

The app does not currently synchronize these saves to an account database. Its optional sign-in screen is inactive when no authentication service is configured.

Hosting and external content

Vercel hosts this site and processes connection information, such as IP addresses, requested URLs and browser information, to deliver and secure it. Demo photographs load from Unsplash image servers, which receive the connection information needed to serve those images. Links to Pinterest, GitHub or other sites open services governed by their own policies. Original demo audio is served with this app.

Far.Fly does not currently include advertising trackers or an analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal information.

Planned Pinterest connection

If enabled and authorized by you, Far.Fly will request read access to your Pinterest boards and Pins so you can choose boards and browse their visuals in the app. The proposed data includes board and Pin identifiers, titles, descriptions, image URLs and links back to Pinterest. Selected visual themes and your in-app choices will be used to suggest a soundtrack.

We do not request your Pinterest password. OAuth credentials will be handled on the server and will not be published in the app's source code or shared with other users. The proposed integration will not publish Pins, modify boards, manage advertising, or use Pinterest content to train AI models. Any external AI processing would require a separate disclosure and choice before being enabled.

Before this connection goes live, this policy will be updated with the actual token storage, retention and disconnection procedures. Until then, Far.Fly does not collect Pinterest access tokens or import Pinterest account data.

Your choices and deletion

You can clear recent recommendation signals using Profile → Clear history. Saved items and preferences remain until you remove them in the app or clear this site's browser storage. Clearing site data in your browser removes all locally saved Far.Fly information on that browser. Copies in other browsers must be cleared separately.

If you authorize a Pinterest connection in a future release, you will also be able to revoke its access through Pinterest's connected-app settings. Revocation does not itself delete data stored in your browser.

Questions and requests

Contact the maintainer through the Far.Fly project issue tracker for privacy questions or to request a private contact channel. GitHub issues are public: do not post passwords, access tokens, private board contents or other sensitive information there.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the effective date. New account integrations will be described before being enabled.
