Privacy policy

Effective September 16, 2026

Far.Fly is an independent visual discovery and music project maintained by rayfie01. This policy covers farfly.vercel.app.

Current status: The public app uses a demo collection. Jamendo music discovery is available when configured; it does not connect to your personal Jamendo account. Music requests send a broad mood tag to our server and Jamendo. Playing tracks and loading artwork connects your browser to Jamendo, which receives your IP address. Liked track metadata and saved soundtracks remain on your device. Pinterest connections are available with your authorization for public boards and Pins.

Information used by the current app

Your saved visuals, likes, saved mood sessions, selected boards and recommendation preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. Recent interactions help suggest music within the app. These themes are not a diagnosis or an assessment of your mental health. Demo image analysis runs locally; the app does not send images or browsing activity to an AI service.

The app does not currently synchronize these saves to an account database. Its optional sign-in screen is inactive when no authentication service is configured.

Hosting and external content

Vercel hosts this site and processes connection information, such as IP addresses, requested URLs and browser information, to deliver and secure it. Demo photographs load from Unsplash image servers, which receive the connection information needed to serve those images. Links to Pinterest, GitHub or other sites open services governed by their own policies. Original demo audio is served with this app.

Far.Fly does not currently include advertising trackers or an analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal information.

Pinterest connection

If enabled and authorized by you, Far.Fly will request read access to your Pinterest boards and Pins so you can choose boards and browse their visuals in the app. The data includes board and Pin identifiers, titles, descriptions, image URLs and links back to Pinterest. Selected visual themes and your in-app choices will be used to suggest a soundtrack.

We do not request your Pinterest password. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted by our server in Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookies scoped to the Pinterest API routes. App JavaScript cannot read them. We renew access automatically while Pinterest authorization remains valid. Logging out of Pinterest in Far.Fly clears these cookies. Pinterest may require reconnection after revocation or prolonged inactivity. Tokens are not stored in a shared database or source code.

Board identifiers you select are kept in session storage so selections survive a refresh in the same tab. Imported Pin content stays in tab memory. Logout clears board selections, imported content and Pinterest activity from the app. Explicitly saved soundtrack sessions may retain derived moods and audio choices, but no Pinterest Pin content. Images load from Pinterest and may be resized and cached by Vercel to extract a local colour palette. No Pinterest images or text are sent to AI services. The integration cannot publish Pins, edit boards or manage advertising.

Your choices and deletion

You can clear recent recommendation signals using Profile → Clear history. Saved items and preferences remain until you remove them in the app or clear this site's browser storage. Clearing site data in your browser removes all locally saved Far.Fly information on that browser. Copies in other browsers must be cleared separately.

Use the Pinterest connection page in Far.Fly to disconnect this browser. This removes Far.Fly's session cookie, but does not revoke the original authorization at Pinterest; you can revoke that separately through Pinterest's connected-app settings. Far.Fly may detect revocation on its next API request. To remove a saved soundtrack session, delete it from Saved.

Questions and requests

Contact the maintainer through the Far.Fly project issue tracker for privacy questions or to request a private contact channel. GitHub issues are public: do not post passwords, access tokens, private board contents or other sensitive information there.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the effective date. New account integrations will be described before being enabled.
